# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Emergency Operations Center personnel use the application on a shared large-screen workstation with a stylus. During active incidents, they need to understand the operational picture quickly and manipulate plans without small controls, hidden actions, or unnecessary navigation.

## Product Purpose

The Command Center provides a shared map-based workspace for monitoring hazards, managing evacuation centers, and preparing operational plans for Camarines Norte. Operational planning is the primary workflow. Success means an operator can move from situational awareness to a clear, publishable plan quickly and confidently.

## Brand Personality

Calm, authoritative, and purposeful. The interface should feel recognizably civic and dependable while retaining enough visual character to stay engaging during sustained use.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy science-fiction command-center styling, decorative dashboards, dense control panels, novelty interactions, and visually flat government software that feels dated or lifeless.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the map and operational plan as the center of attention.
2. Make the next planning action obvious and directly reachable.
3. Prefer large, forgiving controls and visible state over compact density.
4. Use hierarchy and restrained color to communicate urgency without creating noise.
5. Preserve operator confidence with immediate feedback, clear mode boundaries, and safe destructive actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target was specified. The interface should still provide large stylus-friendly targets, strong contrast, visible focus states, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion support as product-quality defaults.
